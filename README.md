# CookBook
Duolingo for cooking. Learn the skills first, then cook your way around the world.  ## What it does - **Skill-based learning.** Practise core techniques like knife work, sautéing, cooking rice and making a roux. - **Skip what you know.** A quick set-up places you as Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced, so experienced cooks can jump straight to new cuisines. - **Recipes tagged by skill.** Every dish shows which skills it uses. Tap a skill to review it. - **Cuisine passport.** Cook a dish, tap "I cooked it", and earn a stamp in that cuisine's passport.  ## Status Early demo. The dishes are hard-coded and nothing is saved between visits. Planned next: AI-generated recipes for any dish, photo recognition, feedback on your finished dish, and 100+ cuisines.
